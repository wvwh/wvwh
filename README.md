### 👋 About Me

Hey, I'm **Kerch** — an OSINT specialist and software developer based in **São Paulo, Brazil 🇧🇷**. I focus on open-source intelligence gathering, digital investigation, and building tools that support cybersecurity and analytical work.

Despite being early in my journey, my skills come from hands-on practice, real projects, and constant self-study rather than theory alone. I'm also interested in **International Relations**, which shapes how I approach investigations — thinking about context, geopolitics, and the bigger picture behind the data.

**Core focus areas:**

- 🔎 **OSINT** — open-source intelligence gathering & digital investigation methodologies
- 🌍 **International Relations** — geopolitical context applied to intelligence analysis
- 🐍 **Python** — scripting, automation, and tooling for investigative workflows
- 🛡️ **Cyber Investigation** — applied cybersecurity principles and digital forensics fundamentals
- 🕵️ **Digital Footprint Analysis** — mapping online presence, metadata, and data correlation
- 🌐 **Web Scraping & Automation** — building scripts to collect and process public data at scale
- 🔐 **Threat Intelligence** — tracking indicators, patterns, and emerging risks from open sources
- 📡 **Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT)** — investigation techniques across social platforms
- 🗺️ **Geolocation & GEOINT** — identifying locations from images, context clues, and metadata
- 📚 **Continuous Research** — always studying new tools, frameworks, and investigative methods

> *Intelligence to defend, never to attack.*
